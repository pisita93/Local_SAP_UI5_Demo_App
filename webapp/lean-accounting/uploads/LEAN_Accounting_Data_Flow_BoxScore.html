LEAN Accounting Data Flow

This page explains how business data moves from planning, sales, production, and financial documents into Lean Accounting reports, helping users understand how daily operations become management insight.

Presentation objective

Help non-SAP users understand why master data design matters. If profit center, material groups, work center, batch, customer country, and channel are maintained correctly, daily transactions can automatically flow into management reporting.

Master data = business labelTransaction = daily activityAnalytics = management view

Simple explanation

A sales order, production confirmation, goods movement, or billing document is not only a document number. It carries labels such as value stream, brand, product type, work center, market, and channel. Analytics reads and groups those labels.

Recommended storyline

  1. Start with business labels: maintain profit center, material, customer, work center, and batch so every transaction carries the right reporting meaning.
  2. Capture the commercial flow: Sales Order → Delivery / GI → Billing captures demand, delivery timing, revenue, market, and channel.
  3. Capture the supply flow: S&OP → Production Plan → Production Confirmation captures planned output, actual output, yield, loss, and capacity by line.
  4. Combine into the SAP data layer: Financial Document, Margin Analysis, CDS views, and capacity views connect financial and operational results.
  5. Consume in Lean Accounting views: Box Score, Analytic Dashboard, and Portfolio / BCG view turn daily activity into management insight and action.

What users should remember

  • Profit center decides the value-stream view.
  • Work center decides production-line and capacity analysis.
  • Material groups decide brand, product type, pack, and portfolio slicing.
  • Customer country and channel decide domestic/export and channel analysis.
  • Batch data supports shelf-life, expiry, recall, FEFO, and quality drill-down.
